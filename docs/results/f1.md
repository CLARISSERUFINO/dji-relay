`RemoteControllerKey.KeyFirmwareVersion` (controller) and
`RemoteControllerKey.KeyFirmwareMatchType` (aircraft family the controller firmware is
built for). Logged with the prefix `firmware:`. Compared with DJI Fly and with the
MSDK 5.20.1 release notes ("Supported Product List and Firmware
Version": DJI Mini 3 Pro `01.00.10.00`, DJI RC N1 `04.14.07.00`).

through DJI Fly to the versions listed for MSDK 5.20.1, and T2–T5 and T7 were repeated.

## Issues found

1. **Wireless adb drops** when the phone is plugged into the RC-N1 or unplugged from it. Wireless
   debugging restarts on a new port; `adb connect adb-<serial>._adb-tls-connect._tcp`
   (mDNS name) reconnects. The app's log stays in the phone buffer, so nothing is lost.
2. **Registration waits while the phone screen is off.** In Doze the app sees no network
   (`network available=false`); `registerApp()` completed only after the phone woke.
   Keep the phone unlocked during tests.
3. **Android's USB chooser** appears on replug instead of the app coming to the front
   directly, until "Always" is ticked for DjiRelay.
4. **Firmware older than the versions listed for MSDK 5.20.1** on the first run (aircraft
   `01.00.0900`, RC `04.14.0042`). All tests passed anyway; the aircraft and RC were then
   updated to `01.00.1000` / `04.14.0700`.
5. **Double registration left the camera list missing.** On one start, the network
   listener called `registerApp()` while the first registration was still running.
   The app registered twice, `onAvailableCameraUpdated` never fired, and the pilot view
   stayed on "Waiting for the drone camera". Fixed in F2: `registerApp()` is not called
   again while a registration is in progress, and the camera-list listener is removed
   and added again on every `onRegisterSuccess` and `onProductConnect`.
