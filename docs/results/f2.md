### SEI (V14)

- The bitstream has **no SEI NAL units**. The SEI comes only as side data in
  `StreamInfo.getSeiList()`.
- Every callback has exactly one entry: `seiType` **240**, payload **25 bytes**.
- First payloads (hex):
  ```
  c4 27 30 00 af 26 2e 00 2f 92 55 ff 17 00 ff b5 88 ff 05 00 c0 c0 00 de 80
  c5 27 30 00 5f 27 2d 00 2f 7c 65 ff 16 00 ff d7 88 ff 05 00 c0 c0 00 de 80
  c6 27 30 00 26 26 31 00 34 5a a6 ff 18 00 ff f8 88 ff 05 00 c0 c0 00 de 80
  ```
- Bytes 0–1 (little-endian u16) advance by 0, 1, 2 or 3 between callbacks (412, 800,
  340 and 2 times) and span 1712 counts over the 1712 callbacks. They are not a strict
  frame counter: the value repeats between different frames.
- Bytes 20–24 are constant. The other fields vary from frame to frame.
- The payload format is not documented by DJI and is not decoded here. It holds no
  field identified as a capture timestamp. The raw payloads stay in `messages.jsonl`.

## E2: `pts_ms` reference (V3)

| Item | Result |
|---|---|
| Clock | **UTC epoch milliseconds** (first value `1795302148607`) |
| Offset | `pts_ms` is about 25 ms *ahead* of the phone's `System.currentTimeMillis()` read when the message is written, so it is not the phone's clock read at arrival |
| Monotonic | Yes: no non-increasing step |
| Step | mean 33.52 ms, sd 10.95 ms; most frequent 29–31 ms and 39–41 ms; min 1, max 147 |
| Rate vs phone `elapsedRealtime` | −27 ppm |
| Relation to arrival | `pts_ms` steps follow the phone's arrival steps (correlation 0.906); sd of `t_rx − pts` 3.74 ms; over the lower envelope p50 8.3 ms, p95 12.9 ms |
| Regular 30 fps grid | Neither `pts_ms` nor `t_rx` fits a regular grid over the callback index (residual sd about 23 ms for both) |

**Reading:** `pts_ms` is a UTC-based timestamp that moves with the arrival times. It does
not behave like a fixed-rate encoder clock, and the frames do not arrive on a regular
30 fps grid. Spec 10.2 step 2 has two options: map `pts_ms` through the lower envelope
(if `pts_ms` is the encoder clock), or fit a regular 30 fps grid. Neither applies
cleanly. The RTP timestamp mode and the live-time correction chosen from these results
are under Decisions.

## Callback time (NFR2)

Copy + enqueue, measured inside the callback (`cb_ns`): p50 43 µs, p95 89 µs, max
703 µs. The UI showed a maximum below 5 ms throughout.

## Phone recording

- Started and stopped from the app button. The pilot view kept running during the
  recording.
- Session events: `session_start`, `session_stop` (reason `user`). No `drop` events.

## Decisions

1. **RTP timestamp = `pts_ms` × 90** (90 kHz clock). `pts_ms` is monotonic and tracks
   arrival within a few ms.
2. **Live-time correction is left to the F4 analysis.** Stage 1's LED fit absorbs the
   constant part of the latency; the remaining jitter of `t_rx − pts` is about 4 ms (sd).
3. **Resolution:** SSL-Vision has no requirement on the frame resolution, so 1440×1080
   is accepted. The live resolution is checked again in F5, once the camera is put in
   video mode (it may follow the camera mode or aspect ratio).

## Open points

1. **SEI type 240** content is unknown. It is kept raw in the messages.

## Issues found

1. FFmpeg reports errors for the callbacks before the first IDR, as expected for a
   recording that starts mid-GOP. Decoders skip them; no change needed.
